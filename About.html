Our story

One borrowed oven, ten years ago.

Paradise Pizza Hub started as a Sunday pop-up out of a friend's backyard oven. The pizza was good enough that people kept showing up — so we built a proper one.

2016 — Today

Built around the fire, not the menu.

We didn't set out to open a restaurant — we set out to get the oven right. Everything else, the dough recipe, the short seasonal menu, the room built around an open kitchen, came after. The oven is still the first thing you see when you walk in, because it's still the reason we're here.

Today Paradise Pizza Hub is run by the same two people who started it, plus a small kitchen crew that's been with us since year two. We buy from three local farms, mill our own semolina blend, and close the oven the moment the wood runs low rather than switch to gas.

What we won't compromise on

Three things we don't negotiate.

Wood

Real fire, always

Oak and almond wood only — no gas assist, ever. It changes the way the crust tastes and we're not willing to lose that.

Sourcing

Grown nearby

Produce comes from three farms within 40 miles. If it's not in season locally, it's not on the menu.

Pace

One pizza at a time

We cap covers on busy nights rather than rush the dough or crowd the oven. Quality drops the second we do that.
